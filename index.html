Biography

Meet Pranee

Some people leave behind achievements.
Others leave behind the way they made people feel.

Francis Lal, known as Lins to his family and Pranee to his friends, was someone whose curiosity quietly shaped the way he lived.

He loved understanding how things worked. Whether it was a machine, a drawing, or a new piece of technology, learning never felt like work—it was simply part of who he was.

Mechanical engineering became his profession, but creativity was always close beside it.

Drawing allowed him to express what words sometimes couldn't.

Music was almost always playing in the background. From old Malayalam classics to English songs and everything in between, every moment seemed to have its own soundtrack.

He enjoyed learning, creating, spending time with family, laughing with friends, and appreciating life's simple moments.

This website is a celebration of that journey.
